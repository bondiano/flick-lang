The go target (`target = "go"`) compiles what `main` reaches, and reports
here a construct it does not compile yet, with what it names: a lazy `for`
that reads services, refined types, or a `foreign` binding without a Go
implementation, in the standard library or beside the program. It also reports
a `main` that does not take one parameter, an app without an entry, and a
package module not available on the go target. The js target builds the same
program.

When the construct is the program's own `foreign` binding, add its Go
implementation beside the program (see the FFI reference). Otherwise keep
the construct out of what `main` reaches, or build with `target = "js"`.

Availability of modules, packages, and Effect-adapter bindings for a selected
environment is reported with `FLICK-P064`.
