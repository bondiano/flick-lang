`let mut` declares a variable of one function: a name, bound by a
statement of a block of its body. A definition of the module, a function,
a pattern, `let assert` and the bindings of `do` and `for` cannot be
`mut`. Bind a pattern with `let`, then the part that changes with a
`let mut` of one name; to change a function, assign a `fun` to a variable.

```flick
module App.Totals exposing (largest)

largest : (Int, Int) -> Int
let largest pair =
  let (a, b) = pair
  let mut best = a
  if b > best then best = b
  best
```
