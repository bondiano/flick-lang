Time to put the chapter together. `fizzbuzz` takes a number and gives back
`Fizz` for a multiple of 3, `Buzz` for a multiple of 5, `FizzBuzz` for a
multiple of both, and the number itself otherwise.

The starter stops after the first case. An `if` without `else` is `Unit`, so
the compiler reports `FLICK-T003`. Finish the function, then press
**Check answer**: the check compiles your code, looks at the exported
signature and compares what `main` prints.
