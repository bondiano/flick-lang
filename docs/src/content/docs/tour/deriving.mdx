<TourLesson locale="en" id="deriving">

`deriving` asks the compiler to write the instances of common classes for a
type: `Eq` for `==`, `Ord` for ordering, `Show` for `show`.

A derived `Ord` compares by the order of the forms, then by the values
inside: hearts come before spades, and two hearts compare by their number.
`List.sort` needs `Ord`, and `compare` gives `LT`, `EQ` or `GT`.

**Try:** take `Ord` off `Card`. The compiler reports `FLICK-C001` at
`List.sort`: there is no instance `Ord Card`.

</TourLesson>
