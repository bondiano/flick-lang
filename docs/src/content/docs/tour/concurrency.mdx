<TourLesson locale="en" id="concurrency">

`parallel` runs two tasks at once and waits for both: the slow and the fast
one finish in the time of the slower. `race_first` runs two and takes the
result of the first to finish, stopping the other. `Effect.sleep` waits
without blocking anything else.

All of it needs `Async` in the signature: these functions suspend while they
wait.

**Try:** remove `Async` from the signature of `main`. The compiler reports
`FLICK-E002`, and its fix puts `Async` back.

</TourLesson>
