<TourLesson locale="en" id="patterns">

Patterns can do more than name a form. In `describe`:

- `[]` is the empty list and `[only]` a list of one element.
- `[0, ..._] | [1, ..._]`: `|` between patterns takes either, and `..._` is
  the rest of the list, ignored.
- `when` adds a condition to an arm, and `...rest` names the rest.

Arms are tried from the top, and the first that fits wins.

**Try:** delete the last arm. The compiler reports `FLICK-T009` and shows a
list that no arm covers: an arm with `when` may not match, so it does not
count.
