<TourLesson locale="en" id="options">

Flick has no `null`. A value that may be missing is an `Option`: `Some value`
or `None`. A step that may fail gives a `Result`: `Ok value` or
`Err problem`.

`Int.parse` gives an `Option Int`, and `age` turns it into a `Result` with a
message. Its `match` takes both apart, and `when n >= 0` refines an arm.

`List.head` of an empty list is `None`, not a crash.

**Try:** print `Int.parse "36" + 1`. The compiler reports `FLICK-T003`: an
`Option Int` is not an `Int` until a `match` or `Option.with_default` takes
the number out.

</TourLesson>
