**Where it runs:** browser and locally.

Two concurrent awaits share the cell, not two copies of the body.
The first line shows one execution. Interruption is not cached: the timeout
interrupts the fiber that owns `slow`, resetting the cell. The next await
runs again after the gate opens; the second line shows two attempts.
Other waiters do not inherit the owner's interruption.

A lazy retains its captures and cached error, so a scoped resource it
captures cannot escape its region. These are observable guarantees, not
instructions to depend on the compiler's lazy optimizations.
