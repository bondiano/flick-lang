**Where it runs:** browser and locally.

`Functor` declares identity and composition laws. Each lemma proves one
for our `Maybe` instance by splitting its two constructors. The `lawful`
bundle names every required lemma; a missing field is `FLICK-T083`.
A class declaration alone does not prove that all its instances are lawful.

The browser checks the proofs and runs `main`; CI also runs `flick prove`
with this module in the proof root. The laws quantify over total functions,
not callbacks that fail or loop.

**Guarantee:** the bundle certifies this instance's declared laws in the
kernel's domain; it neither verifies every instance nor the runtime.
