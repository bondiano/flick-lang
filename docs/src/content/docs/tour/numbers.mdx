<TourLesson locale="en" id="numbers">

`Int` is a whole number and `Float` a fractional one. An operator takes two
numbers of the same type, and Flick never converts one into the other behind
your back: `Int.to_float` does it when you ask.

On `Int`, `/` drops the remainder and `%` gives it. An `Int` has 32 bits and
wraps around: one more than the largest gives the smallest, as the last line
shows.

**Try:** call `add 1 2.5`. The compiler reports `FLICK-T003`: it expected an
`Int` and found a `Float`.

</TourLesson>
