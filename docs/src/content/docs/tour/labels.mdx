<TourLesson locale="en" id="labels">

`continue` skips the rest of one pass of a loop: here, the pairs where `b`
is not greater than `a`. `break` and `continue` act on the nearest loop, the
inner `for`.

To stop both loops at once, name the outer one: `'outer:` before `for` is a
label, and `break 'outer` ends that loop, not the inner one. `continue 'outer`
would go on with its next pass. A label works on `loop` and `while` as well.

The [reference](../../reference/bindings/#local-loops) has the full rules.

**Try:** write `break` in place of `break 'outer`. Only the inner loop ends,
the outer one goes on, and `pair 10` finds `(4, 6)` instead. Write
`break 'other`, and the compiler reports `FLICK-T071`: no loop has that label.

</TourLesson>
