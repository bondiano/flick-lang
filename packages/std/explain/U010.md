A `component` (the plugin `component` of the module `Ui`) declares a view of
its props: its name, one pattern of its props, `=`, then its body. The body
is one expression on the line of `=`, or lines at one column: the
statements of its setup, then the expression that renders it.

```flick
component counter (start : Float) =
  let count = Atom.local start
  ui { text (show (Atom.value count)) }
```

Write the name and the props before `=`, and give the component a body.
